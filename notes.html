Revision

Quick notes and concept reminders

Triangle Rule

Sum of angles in a triangle is 180°. Use area = 1/2 × base × height.

Quadratic Formula

x = [-b ± √(b² − 4ac)] / 2a. Use discriminant to know the nature of roots.

Circle Basics

Area = πr², circumference = 2πr, diameter = 2r.

Probability

P(E) = favorable outcomes / total outcomes. Always count all outcomes carefully.

Statistics

Mean = sum of values / number of values. Median is the middle value in sorted data.

Derivative Rule

If y = xⁿ, then dy/dx = n xⁿ⁻¹. This is the core of differentiation.
