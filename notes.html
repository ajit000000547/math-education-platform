Quick Revision

Notes for fast concept refresh

Triangle Rule

Sum of interior angles in a triangle is 180°, and area is 1/2 × base × height.

Quadratic Formula

x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / 2a, useful when factorization is not direct.

Circle Basics

Area = πr², circumference = 2πr, diameter = 2r.

Probability

P(E) = favorable outcomes / total outcomes. Count carefully before making a decision.

Statistics

Mean = sum / count; median is middle value; mode is most repeated value.

Derivative Rule

If y = xⁿ, then dy/dx = n·xⁿ⁻¹. Differentiation is the rate of change.
