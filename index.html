Solutions

Step-by-step solved examples

Algebra

Solve: 3x + 5 = 20

Step 1: Subtract 5 from both sides.

Step 2: 3x = 15

Step 3: Divide by 3, x = 5

Answer: x = 5

Geometry

Find area of triangle

Formula: Area = 1/2 × base × height

Given: base = 12, height = 8

Area: 1/2 × 12 × 8 = 48

Answer: 48 sq units

Trigonometry

Find tan 45°

Standard value: tan 45° = 1

Explanation: Opposite = Adjacent in a 45° triangle.

Answer: 1

Formulas

Important formula bank

Algebra

  • (a+b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2
  • (a-b)2 = a2 - 2ab + b2
  • a2 - b2 = (a-b)(a+b)

Geometry

  • Area of rectangle = length × breadth
  • Area of circle = πr2
  • Volume of cube = a3

Trigonometry

  • sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse
  • cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse
  • tan θ = opposite / adjacent
