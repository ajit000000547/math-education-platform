Algebra

Solve: 2x + 7 = 19

Step 2: 2x = 12

Geometry

Find area of a triangle

Formula: Area = 1/2 × base × height

Given: base = 10, height = 6

Area: 1/2 × 10 × 6 = 30

Answer: 30 sq units

Trigonometry

Find sin 30°

Using standard values: sin 30° = 1/2

Ratio form: opposite / hypotenuse

Answer: 1/2
